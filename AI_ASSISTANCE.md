# AI assistance disclosure

AI systems were used during the research workflow for drafting, code generation, counterexample search, audit organization, literature-search assistance, and document preparation.

Mathematical claims are not considered validated merely because an AI system produced or endorsed them. The project maintains separate scientific (E), priority / antecedence (N), reproducibility (Q), and publication statuses.

A public-review snapshot should identify which proofs, computations, and editorial text received human review and which automated checks were replayed.
