# Claim boundary

This public-review repository concerns only the prescribed three-element support-translation family `T <-> T+` in finite abelian products `H x C_p` under the hypotheses stated in the theorem witnesses.

It does not claim:

- classification of all cospectral Cayley digraphs of valency 3;
- a general theory of Cayley spectra, provenance, or behavioral equivalence;
- a result concerning the Riemann hypothesis or Hilbert-Polya;
- priority for the general classification while `N = NON AUDITEE`;
- independent human validation of the internal proofs;
- that reconstructed/current reproduction files are original historical execution artifacts;
- that a public `0.1.x` repository is a stable scientific release.

The finite U(210) corpus, the `C_m x C_6` family, and the cyclic examples are supporting context. Known antecedent examples remain identified as such.

## Forbidden public-review formulations

While `N = NON AUDITEE`, public-facing titles, abstracts, repository descriptions, release notes, social summaries, and metadata must not use discovery or priority language that implies precedence. In particular, do not describe the result as a priority classification, an unprecedented family, or the earliest instance of a phenomenon.

Any later priority statement requires a separately documented antecedence audit and an explicit governance change; it cannot be inferred from publication date, repository visibility, proof status, or a negative literature search.
