Let H be a finite abelian group generated by distinct elements kappa and kappa-prime, and let p be an odd prime. In H times F_p, consider the support T = {(kappa,0), (kappa,1), (kappa-prime,0)} and its translate T-plus = T + (0,1). For p at least five, the associated Cayley digraphs are cospectral exactly when there is a homomorphism h from H to F_p with h(kappa-prime) = 1 and h(kappa) equal to 1 or 2. This condition yields an explicit group automorphism between the two supports. For p = 3, the same criterion applies when 3 divides the order of H. When 3 does not divide the order of H, cospectrality is equivalent to invariance of the character-value subgroup Gamma = {(psi(kappa), psi(kappa-prime)): psi is a character of H} under (u,v) mapping to (-v,-u). The arguments use short vanishing sums of roots of unity for p at least five and a unit-circle chord argument for p = 3. We also give formulations in terms of the relation lattice and identify a previously studied circulant example within this family.
