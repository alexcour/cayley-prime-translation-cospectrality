## A short root-of-unity lemma

For any root of unity z with ord(z)>3 and arbitrary roots of unity A,B,C (with repetitions and orders unrestricted),

    A+B+C=2+z  if and only if  {A,B,C}={1,1,z}.

Similarly, A+B+C=1+2z forces the multiset {1,z,z}. These are deductions from the Poonen–Rubinstein (1998) classification, not a new classification.

Proof: the vanishing six-term multiset (A,B,C,-1,-1,-z) has minimal component weights only 2,3,5,6. Weight 5 leaves a nonzero singleton; a minimal component of weight 6 has distinct entries and is excluded by repeated -1. Thus its decomposition consists of 2+2+2 or 3+3. In the 3+3 case the two -1 terms lie in separate triples, and the triple also containing -z would force z to have order three. Therefore three cancelling pairs force {A,B,C}={1,1,z}. For the companion identity divide all terms by z and replace z by z^-1.

The order threshold matters: at order 2, {1,u,-u} is also possible for arbitrary u; at order 3, {-z,-z^2,-z^2} is a noncanonical solution.

## Proposed extension to all n >= 4

Let n>=4, H finite abelian generated by distinct k and kp, G=H x C_n, and define

    T={(k,0),(k,1),(kp,0)};  Tplus=T+(0,1).

Conjecture promoted to a complete internal proof in this note (external review pending): the directed Cayley graphs are adjacency-cospectral, with algebraic multiplicity, exactly when a homomorphism h:H->C_n exists with h(kp)=1 and h(k) in {1,2}. In either case they are isomorphic by an explicit automorphism.

Necessity: write Gamma={(psi(k),psi(kp)): psi in Hhat}, a finite subgroup of S^1 x S^1. Let z be primitive of order n. The eigenvalue 2+z for T, from the trivial H-character and phase z, must occur for Tplus. Writing the Tplus eigenvalue as A+B+C with A=q*u, B=q^2*u, C=q*v, q in mu_n and (u,v) in Gamma, the first short-root lemma gives exactly these character/phase alternatives:

    (q;u,v)=(z;z^-1,z^-1), (z^-1;z^2,z), (1;1,z).

The first two yield a point (z,z) or (z^2,z) in Gamma (invert the first pair if needed). In the third case (1,z) is in Gamma. Hence phase z in T yields eigenvalue 1+2z. Match against Tplus; the companion lemma gives:

    (q;u,v)=(1;z,1), (z^-1;z^2,z^2), (z;z^-1,1).

Respectively take (z,1)*(1,z)=(z,z), or (z^2,z^2)*(1,z)^-1=(z^2,z), or (z^-1,1)^-2*(1,z)=(z^2,z). The middle line bypasses division by 2 modulo n, and therefore also works when n is even. Because k,kp generate H, the resulting character H->mu_n is uniquely identified by its values; taking logarithms in mu_n gives h as required.

Sufficiency: if h(k)=1, the group automorphism (x,y)->(x,y+h(x)) sends T to Tplus. If h(k)=2 use (x,y)->(x,h(x)-y). This proves graph isomorphism and hence cospectrality.

## Evidence and limitations

Exact integer cyclotomic tests: 63 ambient orders N=1..60,72,90,120, total 1,081,309 unordered ambient triples; all 1,970 targets z with ord(z)>3 have only the prescribed solutions. Separate Cayley tests cover 4,928 generator presentations across n=4,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,12,14,15 with 102 positive cases and zero mismatches with direct homomorphism enumeration. These finite counts do NOT prove the general theorem. Complete scripts/results are in the programme evidence pack dated 2026-10-20; the source repository review PR should contain executable code.

Review needed: verify necessity permutations and identity of Gamma over composite/even n; run an independent implementation; establish earlier literature; check differences from the separately treated n=3 case. The original manuscript already excluded a weight-five minimal vanishing component; an abbreviated correspondence omitted that detail.

Reference: B. Poonen and M. Rubinstein, *The Number of Intersection Points Made by the Diagonals of a Regular Polygon*, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 11 (1998), 133–156, DOI: 10.1137/S0895480195281246.
