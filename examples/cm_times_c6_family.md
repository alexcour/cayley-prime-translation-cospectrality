# The C_m x C_6 family

For even `m`, define

`A_m = {(1,0),(1,1),(1,3)}`

and

`B_m = {(1,1),(1,2),(1,4)}`

in `C_m x C_6`.

The internal dossier proves cospectrality for every even `m` and isomorphism iff `3 | m`. The two non-cyclic U(210) collisions occur at `m=2` and `m=4`.

Priority for this exact family is not yet closed; no priority claim is made here.


The written historical proof/certificate note is bundled as `reproducibility/legacy_support/PROOF_FAMILY.txt`; the associated archived certificate ZIP is bundled in the same directory.
